Today was a relaxed but special day: it was my birthday! I took the day off work and still
managed to spend a couple of hours studying at Launch School.

I didn't get to study on Monday, August 10, but I made up for it today. To finish the last
chapter, I answered the end-of-chapter questions without looking at my notes. I referred
back to the relevant sections only when I got stuck.

After finishing the questions, I completed an active recall session using the questions I
had created while reading. I was able to answer most of them entirely from memory, with
only a couple of exceptions.

Once I finished, I gave my answers to LSBot for review. The feedback was fairly positive.
LSBot found that I had a strong understanding of the material, though some of my answers
lacked precision. I made the necessary corrections and added the revised answers to Mochi
as flashcards.

Moving forward, I plan to practice my flashcards every day.

Next stop: the Variables chapter.
