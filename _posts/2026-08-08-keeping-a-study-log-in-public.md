A short note to start, since this is the first entry.

I've kept study notes privately for years. This time I'm putting the day-to-day in the
open — not as a highlight reel, but as an honest record of the work. The plan is simple:
after a study session, I write a few lines here about what I was working on, what finally
made sense, and where I'm still stuck.

Two reasons it's public. First, writing for someone else forces a clarity that writing for
myself doesn't. If I can't explain what clicked, I probably haven't understood it yet — and
that gap is exactly what mastery-based learning is meant to close. Second, I've been helped
by other people who learned in the open, and this is a small way to pay that forward.

Don't expect polish. Some entries will be a breakthrough; some will be "low-focus day, did
the work anyway." Both belong here. The record matters more than the streak.

If you're on a similar path, welcome. Let's get to it.
