The path

Infrastructure and systems are my core craft. This is how I'm going deeper: KodeKloud's DevOps Engineer path in four phases, each with a certification and a clear "done when." I build every step in my home lab. Certifications I haven't passed are listed as goals, not credentials.

The end goal is to be an experienced infrastructure engineer with a specialty on top: running and securing AI agents. That specialty runs with Phases 2 to 4 as three agentic stages, and each stage starts only after its phase's certifications are passed.
