The journey

Mastery over speed

I chose Launch School because it matches how I've always learned best — taking things apart, understanding how they work, and rebuilding from first principles. Years in infrastructure taught me that shortcuts usually cost more later, and its mastery-based path rewards the same instinct: understand the ideas, don't just recognize the answers. This is the road from my first Ruby program, through Core, to Capstone.
